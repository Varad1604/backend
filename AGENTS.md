
## KPMPL Mandatory Task Reporting

After EVERY coding task, always provide a readable final report in chat.

Required sections:
1. TASK SUMMARY - What was accomplished.
2. FILES CHANGED - Exact paths and changes.
3. TEST RESULTS - Commands, test counts, PASS/FAIL and exit codes.
4. ERRORS/BLOCKERS - Unresolved problems.
5. CURRENT STATUS - COMPLETE, PARTIAL, FAILED or BLOCKED.
6. NEXT STEPS - Exact instructions for the user.

Never claim completion from a failed test.
Do not repeat a failing command more than twice without a specific new diagnosis.
Always inspect complete error output.
Never end without a human-readable final summary.
Preserve existing uncommitted work.
Do not contact live DataMan, deploy, or restart running services without authorization.
